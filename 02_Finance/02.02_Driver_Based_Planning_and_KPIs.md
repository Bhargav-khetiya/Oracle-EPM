# SECTION 1: WHAT IS DRIVER-BASED PLANNING

---

## The Problem with Traditional Budgeting

```
Traditional Budgeting:
  CFO: "What's your revenue budget for January?"
  Hotel Manager: "₹2 Cr"
  CFO: "How did you get that number?"
  Hotel Manager: "Last year was ₹1.8 Cr... so I added 10%... roughly ₹2 Cr"
  CFO: "What happens if occupancy drops 5%?"
  Hotel Manager: "...I don't know. I'd have to redo everything."
```

## Driver-Based Planning: The Solution

```
Driver-Based Planning:
  CFO: "What are your assumptions for January?"
  Hotel Manager:
    "Rooms Available:   200 rooms × 31 days = 6,200 room nights
     Occupancy Rate:    78%
     Average Room Rate: ₹3,200"
  
  System calculates:
    Rooms Sold     = 6,200 × 78% = 4,836
    Room Revenue   = 4,836 × ₹3,200 = ₹1,54,75,200
  
  CFO: "What if occupancy drops to 70%?"
  → Change ONE cell (78% → 70%) → Revenue = ₹1,38,88,000
  → INSTANT "what-if" analysis!
```

### The Key Principle

```
  Traditional:  User inputs THE ANSWER     (Revenue = ₹2 Cr)
  Driver-Based: User inputs ASSUMPTIONS    (Volume, Rate, %)
                System CALCULATES the answer

  → Auditable: You can trace WHY revenue = ₹2Cr
  → Flexible:  Change one assumption → entire P&L recalculates
  → Accurate:  Based on operational reality, not gut feel
```

# SECTION 2: WHY DRIVERS MATTER

---

## Driver Types

| Driver Type | What It Is | Examples |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| **Volume Driver** | Count of units/transactions | Rooms Sold, Covers Served, Headcount |
| **Rate Driver** | Price per unit | Avg Room Rate, Avg Salary, Cost per Sq Ft |
| **Percentage Driver** | Ratio or percentage | Occupancy %, Benefit Rate %, Tax Rate % |
| **Calendar Driver** | Time-based | Days in Month, Working Days, Seasonal Factor |

## The Driver Tree Concept

```
REVENUE DRIVER TREE — Hotel Pvt Ltd

Total Revenue
├── Room Revenue = Rooms Available × Occupancy Rate × ADR × Days in Month
│   ├── Rooms Available (input: 200)
│   ├── Occupancy Rate % (input: 75%)
│   ├── ADR - Average Daily Rate (input: ₹3,200)
│   └── Days in Month (system: 31 for Jan)
│
├── F&B Revenue = Covers per Day × Avg Spend per Cover × Days in Month
│   ├── Covers per Day (input: 250)
│   ├── Avg Spend per Cover (input: ₹600)
│   └── Days in Month (system: 31)
│
├── Banquet Revenue = Number of Events × Avg Revenue per Event
│   ├── Number of Events (input: 12)
│   └── Avg Revenue per Event (input: ₹2,33,333)
│
└── Other Revenue = Fixed estimate per month (input: ₹4,00,000)


EXPENSE DRIVER TREE — Hotel Pvt Ltd

Total Expense
├── Salary Expense = Headcount × Avg Salary per Head
│   ├── Headcount (input: 160)
│   └── Avg Salary (input: ₹32,500)
│
├── Benefits = Salary Expense × Benefits Rate %
│   └── Benefits Rate (input: 25%)
│
├── Food Cost = F&B Revenue × Food Cost %
│   └── Food Cost % (input: 35%)
│
├── Utilities = Square Footage × Cost per Sq Ft
│   ├── Square Footage (input: 50,000)
│   └── Cost per Sq Ft (input: ₹17)
│
├── OTA Commission = Room Revenue × Commission Rate %
│   └── Commission Rate (input: 15%)
│
└── Marketing = Revenue × Marketing Spend %
    └── Marketing Spend % (input: 5%)
```

# SECTION 3: HOTEL REVENUE DRIVER MODEL

---

## Worked Example: January FY26

```
ROOM REVENUE CALCULATION
─────────────────────────
Input:  Rooms Available       = 200
Input:  Days in January       = 31
Calc:   Total Room Nights     = 200 × 31 = 6,200
Input:  Occupancy Rate        = 75%
Calc:   Rooms Sold            = 6,200 × 75% = 4,650
Input:  Average Daily Rate    = ₹2,581
Calc:   Room Revenue          = 4,650 × ₹2,581 = ₹1,20,01,650
                                                  ≈ ₹1,20,00,000

F&B REVENUE CALCULATION
─────────────────────────
Input:  Covers per Day        = 250
Input:  Avg Spend per Cover   = ₹580
Input:  Days in January       = 31
Calc:   F&B Revenue           = 250 × ₹580 × 31 = ₹44,95,000
                                                    ≈ ₹45,00,000

BANQUET REVENUE CALCULATION
─────────────────────────────
Input:  Events in January     = 12
Input:  Avg Revenue per Event = ₹2,33,333
Calc:   Banquet Revenue       = 12 × ₹2,33,333 = ₹28,00,000

TOTAL REVENUE = ₹1,20,00,000 + ₹45,00,000 + ₹28,00,000 + ₹8,00,000 + ₹4,00,000
             = ₹2,05,00,000 ✓ (matches our P&L!)
```

### Seasonal Variation

```
Occupancy Rate by Month:
  Jan: 75%  Feb: 72%  Mar: 68%  ← Winter (conferences)
  Apr: 65%  May: 55%  Jun: 50%  ← Summer (low season)
  Jul: 52%  Aug: 48%  Sep: 58%  ← Monsoon (lowest)
  Oct: 78%  Nov: 85%  Dec: 82%  ← Festival season (peak!)

ADR by Season:
  Peak (Oct-Jan):    ₹3,500
  Mid (Feb-Apr):     ₹2,800  
  Low (May-Sep):     ₹2,000

→ Revenue varies DRAMATICALLY by month
→ Driver-based planning captures this; "₹2Cr/month flat" doesn't
```

# SECTION 5: BUILDING DRIVER MODELS IN EPBCS

---

## Account Dimension Design for Drivers

```
Account Hierarchy (Hotel Revenue section):
  Revenue [+] (Dynamic Calc — sum of children)
  ├── Room_Revenue [~] (Dynamic Calc — formula: Rooms_Sold × ADR)
  │   ├── Rooms_Available (Store — user input)
  │   ├── Occupancy_Rate (Store — user input, data type: percentage)
  │   ├── Rooms_Sold [~] (Dynamic Calc — formula: Rooms_Available × Occ_Rate)
  │   └── ADR (Store — user input)
  │
  ├── FnB_Revenue [~] (Dynamic Calc — formula: Covers × Avg_Spend × Days)
  │   ├── Covers_Per_Day (Store — user input)
  │   └── Avg_Spend_Per_Cover (Store — user input)
  │
  └── Banquet_Revenue [~] (Dynamic Calc — formula: Events × Avg_Event_Rev)
      ├── Num_Events (Store — user input)
      └── Avg_Event_Revenue (Store — user input)
```

### Member Formulas for Drivers

```essbase
/* Member: Rooms_Sold (Dynamic Calc) */
IF("Rooms_Available" <> #Missing AND "Occupancy_Rate" <> #Missing)
    "Rooms_Available" * "Occupancy_Rate" / 100;
ELSE
    #Missing;
ENDIF;

/* Member: Room_Revenue (Dynamic Calc) */
"Rooms_Sold" * "ADR";

/* Member: FnB_Revenue (Dynamic Calc) */
"Covers_Per_Day" * "Avg_Spend_Per_Cover" * 
    @ATTRIBUTEVAL("Days_In_Month");
/* Or use a substitution variable for days */

/* Member: Total_Revenue (Dynamic Calc) */
/* Uses consolidation operator (+) on children — no formula needed */
```

### Form Design for Driver Input

```
Form: "Revenue_Drivers"

POV: Scenario=Budget, Version=Working, Year=FY26, Entity={RTP_Entity}

Layout:
  ROWS: Driver Accounts (Rooms_Available, Occ_Rate, ADR, Covers, etc.)
  COLUMNS: Jan, Feb, Mar, ..., Dec, YearTotal

  Type: 
    Rooms_Available → Integer (no decimals)
    Occupancy_Rate  → Percentage (2 decimals)
    ADR             → Currency (0 decimals)

  Calculated members shown as READ-ONLY (gray background):
    Rooms_Sold, Room_Revenue, Total_Revenue
```

## Hotel KPIs — Complete Reference

### Revenue KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Status |
|-----|---------|--------|--------|
| **RevPAR** | Room Revenue / Available Rooms | > ₹2,000 | Revenue efficiency |
| **ADR** | Room Revenue / Rooms Sold | > ₹3,000 | Pricing power |
| **Occupancy %** | Rooms Sold / Rooms Available | > 70% | Demand |
| **TRevPAR** | Total Revenue / Available Rooms | > ₹3,000 | Total efficiency |

### Profitability KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Status |
|-----|---------|--------|--------|
| **Gross Margin %** | Gross Profit / Revenue | > 80% | Service margin |
| **EBITDA Margin %** | EBITDA / Revenue | > 30% | Operating profitability |
| **Net Margin %** | Net Income / Revenue | > 12% | Bottom line |
| **GOPPAR** | GOP / Available Rooms | > ₹1,000 | Profit per room |

### Efficiency KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target | Status |
|-----|---------|--------|--------|
| **Labour Cost %** | Labour / Revenue | < 35% | Workforce efficiency |
| **Food Cost %** | Food Cost / F&B Revenue | < 35% | Kitchen efficiency |
| **CPOR** | Total Cost / Rooms Sold | < ₹2,500 | Cost efficiency |
| **Revenue per Employee** | Revenue / Headcount | > ₹1.2L | Productivity |

### All as Member Formulas

```essbase
/* RevPAR */
IF("Rooms_Available" <> 0 AND "Rooms_Available" <> #Missing)
    "Room_Revenue" / "Rooms_Available";
ELSE #Missing; ENDIF;

/* ADR */
IF("Rooms_Sold" <> 0 AND "Rooms_Sold" <> #Missing)
    "Room_Revenue" / "Rooms_Sold";
ELSE #Missing; ENDIF;

/* EBITDA Margin % */
IF("Revenue" <> 0 AND "Revenue" <> #Missing)
    "EBITDA" / "Revenue" * 100;
ELSE #Missing; ENDIF;

/* Labour Cost % */
IF("Revenue" <> 0 AND "Revenue" <> #Missing)
    ("Salary_Expense" + "Benefits_Expense") / "Revenue" * 100;
ELSE #Missing; ENDIF;
```

# SECTION 7: WHAT-IF ANALYSIS

---

## The Power of Driver-Based Planning

```
SCENARIO COMPARISON — Hotel India FY26

                        Base Case    Optimistic   Pessimistic
INPUTS:
  Occupancy Rate        75%          82%          60%
  ADR                   ₹2,581       ₹2,800      ₹2,200
  Headcount             160          165          145

CALCULATED OUTPUTS:
  Rooms Sold            4,650        5,084        3,720
  Room Revenue          ₹1.20 Cr    ₹1.42 Cr    ₹0.82 Cr
  Total Revenue         ₹2.05 Cr    ₹2.35 Cr    ₹1.55 Cr
  Salary Expense        ₹0.52 Cr    ₹0.54 Cr    ₹0.47 Cr
  EBITDA                ₹0.68 Cr    ₹0.92 Cr    ₹0.28 Cr
  Net Income            ₹0.33 Cr    ₹0.53 Cr    ₹0.02 Cr

→ Change 3 inputs → entire P&L recalculates in seconds
→ CFO sees: "If occupancy drops to 60%, we barely break even"
→ Decision: "We need to increase marketing OR reduce headcount"
```

### EPBCS Implementation

```
Scenario Dimension:
  ├── Budget          ← Base case
  ├── Forecast        ← Updated prediction
  ├── Optimistic      ← Best case what-if
  └── Pessimistic     ← Worst case what-if

Business Rule: "Copy_Budget_to_Scenarios"
  DATACOPY Budget TO Optimistic;
  DATACOPY Budget TO Pessimistic;
  
  FIX("Optimistic")
      "Occupancy_Rate" = 82;
      "ADR" = 2800;
  ENDFIX
  
  FIX("Pessimistic")  
      "Occupancy_Rate" = 60;
      "ADR" = 2200;
  ENDFIX
```

# SECTION 8: COMMON MISTAKES

---

| Mistake | Problem | Correct Approach |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Making ALL accounts driver-based | Over-engineered; some expenses are just flat amounts | Only use drivers where there's a clear mathematical relationship |
| Circular references in drivers | Revenue depends on Marketing, Marketing depends on Revenue | Break the cycle: Marketing = PRIOR month Revenue × % |
| Putting drivers and calculated values in same form | Users try to edit calculated cells | Separate "Input" forms from "Review" forms |
| Not handling #Missing in driver formulas | 200 × #Missing = #Missing | Always add IF guards in member formulas |
| Ignoring seasonality | Same occupancy rate for all 12 months | Allow month-by-month driver input |
| Hard-coding days in month | Every month = 30 days | Use calendar attribute or OOTB days-in-month |

# SECTION 9: INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

---

### Beginner

**Q1: What is driver-based planning?**
> Instead of users typing final numbers, they input assumptions (volume, rate, percentage). The system calculates the financial result. Example: Users enter Occupancy Rate and ADR; system calculates Room Revenue.

**Q2: What is RevPAR?**
> Revenue Per Available Room = Room Revenue / Rooms Available. It measures hotel revenue efficiency regardless of occupancy. A hotel can have low occupancy but high ADR and still have good RevPAR.

### Intermediate

**Q3: How do you handle seasonality in driver-based planning?**
> Allow drivers to be entered at the monthly level (not annual). Create seasonal factor members or let users input different occupancy rates for each month. The system calculates monthly revenue from monthly drivers.

**Q4: How do you prevent circular references in driver models?**
> Classic example: Marketing = Revenue × 5%, but Revenue depends on marketing-driven occupancy. Solution: Use PRIOR period revenue for the marketing calculation, or fix marketing as a flat amount, or use an iterative calculation approach.

### Architect

**Q5: A client wants 3 versions of their plan: Base, Upside, Downside. How do you architect this?**
> Option 1: Use the Version dimension (Base, Upside, Downside). Option 2: Use the Scenario dimension. Option 3: Create a custom "What-If" dimension. I prefer Option 1 (Version) because it keeps scenarios clean for Budget/Actual/Forecast, and versions can be compared on the same form. Create a DATACOPY rule to seed Upside/Downside from Base, then let users modify only the driver inputs.
