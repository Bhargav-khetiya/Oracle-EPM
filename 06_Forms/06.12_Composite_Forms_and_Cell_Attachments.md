# Composite Forms and Cell Attachments

Beyond standard layouts, Forms can be layered and augmented with unstructured data to support complex business processes.

## 1. Composite Forms (Master-Detail Syncing)

A Composite Form is a wrapper that displays multiple individual forms on a single screen.

*   **The Problem:** You have a high-level P&L form, and a detailed Headcount input form. The user wants to see how changing headcount impacts the P&L on the same screen.
*   **The Solution:** Build a Composite Form with Form A on top and Form B on the bottom.
*   **Master-Detail Sync (The Magic):** You can link the POV of both forms. 
    *   Set Form A's Entity dimension to act as the "Master".
    *   Set Form B's Entity dimension to act as the "Detail".
    *   When the user selects `IT_Dept` on Form A, Form B instantly refreshes to show `IT_Dept` data.

*(Note: Oracle is actively migrating customers away from legacy Composite Forms toward Dashboards 2.0, which provide identical syncing functionality but with a much more modern Redwood UI).*

## 2. Cell Attachments and Unstructured Data

Essbase natively only stores numbers. But finance processes require context. Why did travel expenses spike 200%?

*   **Cell Text:** You can attach a text string directly to a data intersection (e.g., `Actual`, `Jan`, `Travel_Exp`). The user right-clicks the cell, selects "Cell Text", and types: *"Emergency trip to London office."*
*   **Document Attachments:** You can upload physical files (PDFs, Excel sheets, Word docs) and pin them to a cell. This is critical for Account Reconciliation (ARCS) or Audit trails where proof of a journal entry must be provided.
*   **Formatting Implications:** 
    *   Cells with attachments display a small visual indicator (a dog-ear in the corner of the cell).
    *   *Warning:* Massive amounts of cell text and attachments are stored relationally (outside the Essbase cube). While Essbase scales perfectly, overloading the relational repository with gigabytes of PDFs attached to cells will drastically slow down the application interface.
