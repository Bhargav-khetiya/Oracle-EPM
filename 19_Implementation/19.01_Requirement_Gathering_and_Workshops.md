# 19.02 — Requirement Gathering and Workshops: Complete Guide

> **Module:** 19_Implementation | **Topic:** Discovery, Stakeholder Workshops, BRD
> **Audience:** Consultant / Business Analyst

---

## 1. WHY — Why Workshops Are Critical

Bad requirements → bad design → expensive rework. A ₹30L project becomes ₹50L when requirements are missed. Two weeks of focused workshops with the right stakeholders saves 4+ weeks of rework.

---

## 2. WORKSHOP STRUCTURE

### Workshop 1: Executive Vision (2 hours)

```
Attendees: CFO, VP Finance, CEO (optional)
Goal: Understand WHY they need EPBCS

Questions:
  1. What are the top 3 pain points with current budgeting?
  2. What does "good" look like? (KPIs, cycle time targets)
  3. What decisions does the CFO need to make faster?
  4. Who are the key users and what are their roles?
  5. What's the budget and timeline expectation?

Output:
  - Project goals documented
  - Success criteria defined
  - Executive sponsor identified
```

### Workshop 2: Planning Process (4 hours)

```
Attendees: FP&A team, Controllers, Budget Coordinators
Goal: Map the current planning process end-to-end

Questions:
  1. Walk me through a typical budget cycle (month by month)
  2. Who enters data? In what format? How often?
  3. What reports does the CFO see?
  4. How do you consolidate across entities?
  5. What's your forecast methodology?
  6. How do you handle currency conversion?
  7. What's your approval workflow?

Output:
  - Process flow diagram (current state)
  - Pain points per process step
  - Data flow map (sources → EPBCS → reports)
```

### Workshop 3: Dimension Design (4 hours)

```
Attendees: FP&A lead, IT lead, Consultant
Goal: Define every dimension and member

Questions per dimension:
  ACCOUNT: What do you track? Revenue line items? Expense categories?
  ENTITY:  What entities report? Hotels? Departments? Cost centers?
  PERIOD:  Monthly? Weekly? What fiscal year start month?
  SCENARIO: Actual vs Budget vs Forecast? Rolling forecast?
  VERSION:  Working vs Final? How many draft versions?
  CUSTOM:   Any extra dimensions? Product line? Channel? Room type?

Output:
  - Dimension member lists (Excel template)
  - Hierarchy diagrams
  - Property assignments (Account Type, Data Storage, Time Balance)
```

### Workshop 4: Forms and Reports (3 hours)

```
Attendees: End users (planners), Controllers, FP&A
Goal: Define every form and report needed

Activity:
  1. Show sample EPBCS forms → "Is this what you need?"
  2. Collect existing Excel templates → map to EPBCS forms
  3. Define: Rows, Columns, Page, POV for each form
  4. Define: Read-only vs Editable, by whom
  5. Define: Dashboards and KPI tiles

Output:
  - Form inventory (name, purpose, layout, audience)
  - Dashboard mockups (Excel or PowerPoint)
  - Reporting matrix (who needs what, how often)
```

### Workshop 5: Rules and Integration (3 hours)

```
Attendees: FP&A lead, IT/data team, Consultant
Goal: Define calculations and data feeds

Questions:
  RULES:
    1. What calculations happen during budget cycle?
    2. What validations should block bad data?
    3. What allocations are needed?
    4. What currency conversions?
  
  DATA:
    1. Where does actual data come from? (ERP, PMS, GL)
    2. What format? (CSV, API, database)
    3. How often? (daily, weekly, monthly)
    4. Who owns the source data?

Output:
  - Business rule inventory (name, logic, trigger)
  - Data flow map (source → transformation → EPBCS)
  - Integration architecture diagram
```

## 4. SHOW ME — Hotel Pvt Ltd Workshop Outcomes

```
Workshop 1 (CFO):
  Pain: "I get consolidated numbers 7 days after month-end"
  Goal: "Same-day consolidated P&L with drill-down"
  KPIs: RevPAR, Occupancy, Profit Margin per hotel

Workshop 2 (Process):
  Current: Excel → email → consolidate → 5 days
  Target: EPBCS → real-time → automated → same day

Workshop 3 (Dimensions):
  Account: 200 members (Revenue × 15, Expenses × 20, KPIs × 10, ...)
  Entity: 12 members (5 hotels + Corporate + regions)
  Custom: Department (8: Front Office, F&B, Spa, ...)

Workshop 4 (Forms):
  35 forms identified:
    15 data entry (Revenue, Expense, Headcount per hotel)
    10 summary/dashboard (P&L, KPI, Variance)
    5 admin (Exchange rates, SubVars, Status tracking)
    5 workforce (Headcount, Salary, Benefits)

Workshop 5 (Rules):
  15 rules identified:
    5 calculation (Revenue, Expense, PnL, KPI, Currency)
    3 data movement (Seed Budget, Copy Version, Rolling Forecast)
    2 allocation (Corporate Cost, Shared Services)
    2 validation (Revenue > 0, Occupancy ≤ 100)
    3 aggregation + automation
```
