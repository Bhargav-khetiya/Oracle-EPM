# 20.01 — Manufacturing Industry Design Pattern

> **Module:** 20_Projects | **Topic:** EPBCS for Manufacturing Companies
> **Audience:** Consultant / Architect

---

## 1. WHY — Manufacturing ≠ Hotels

Hotels plan by rooms/occupancy. Manufacturing plans by product units, BOM (Bill of Materials), production capacity, and raw material costs. The dimension structure, KPIs, and calculation logic are fundamentally different.

---

## 2. DIMENSION DESIGN

```
Account Dimension (Manufacturing-specific):
  Total_Revenue
  ├── Product_Sales          (Units × Price)
  ├── Service_Revenue
  └── Other_Revenue
  Total_COGS
  ├── Raw_Materials          (Units × BOM cost)
  ├── Direct_Labor           (Hours × Rate)
  ├── Manufacturing_Overhead
  └── Freight_Inbound
  Gross_Profit               (Revenue - COGS)
  Operating_Expenses
  ├── SGA                    (Selling, General & Admin)
  ├── R&D
  └── Depreciation
  EBITDA
  Net_Income

  Driver Accounts (Saved Assumptions):
  ├── Unit_Price
  ├── Units_Sold
  ├── Units_Produced
  ├── Raw_Material_Cost_Per_Unit
  ├── Labor_Hours_Per_Unit
  ├── Labor_Rate_Per_Hour
  ├── Capacity_Utilization_%
  ├── Scrap_Rate_%
  └── Safety_Stock_Days

Entity Dimension:
  Company_HQ
  ├── Plant_Pune (production facility)
  ├── Plant_Chennai (production facility)
  ├── Sales_North (sales region)
  ├── Sales_South (sales region)
  └── Corporate

Custom Dimension — Product:
  All_Products
  ├── Product_A (Widget Standard)
  ├── Product_B (Widget Premium)
  ├── Product_C (Component X)
  └── Product_D (Component Y)
```

## 3. KEY CALCULATIONS

```essbase
/* Manufacturing Revenue = Units × Price */
FIX("Budget", "Working", &CurrYear)
  FIX(@RELATIVE("All_Products", 0), @RELATIVE("All_Plants", 0))
    "Product_Sales" = "Units_Sold" * "Unit_Price";
  ENDFIX
ENDFIX

/* COGS = Units Produced × (Material + Labor + Overhead) */
FIX("Budget", "Working", &CurrYear)
  FIX(@RELATIVE("All_Products", 0), @RELATIVE("All_Plants", 0))
    "Raw_Materials" = "Units_Produced" * "Raw_Material_Cost_Per_Unit";
    "Direct_Labor" = "Units_Produced" * "Labor_Hours_Per_Unit" * "Labor_Rate_Per_Hour";
    "Manufacturing_Overhead" = ("Raw_Materials" + "Direct_Labor") * 0.15;
    "Total_COGS" = "Raw_Materials" + "Direct_Labor" + "Manufacturing_Overhead" + "Freight_Inbound";
  ENDFIX
ENDFIX

/* Capacity Utilization */
"Capacity_Utilization" = "Units_Produced" / "Max_Capacity" * 100;

/* Scrap-Adjusted Production */
"Effective_Units" = "Units_Produced" * (1 - "Scrap_Rate" / 100);
```

---

## 4. MANUFACTURING KPIs

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **Gross Margin** | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100 | > 35% |
| **Capacity Utilization** | Units Produced / Max Capacity × 100 | 75-90% |
| **Scrap Rate** | Scrapped Units / Total Produced × 100 | < 3% |
| **Unit Cost** | Total COGS / Units Produced | Minimize |
| **Inventory Days** | Inventory / Daily COGS | 30-45 days |
| **Labor Efficiency** | Standard Hours / Actual Hours × 100 | > 95% |

---

## 5. HOW IT DIFFERS FROM HOTEL PVT LTD

| Aspect | Hotel Pvt Ltd | Manufacturing Co |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Revenue driver | Occupancy × ADR | Units × Price |
| Cost driver | Labor + Utilities | Raw Material + Labor |
| Custom dimension | Department | Product |
| Key KPI | RevPAR | Gross Margin |
| Complexity | Medium | High (BOM, capacity) |
| Allocation | Corporate overhead | Manufacturing overhead |
