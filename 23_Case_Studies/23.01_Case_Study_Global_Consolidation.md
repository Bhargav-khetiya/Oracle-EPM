# Case Study: Global Statutory Consolidation (FCCS)

This case study analyzes a real-world scenario of a multinational corporation implementing Oracle FCCS (Financial Consolidation and Close) to solve complex currency and elimination challenges.

## Background
*   **Company:** GlobalTech Inc.
*   **Structure:** Parent company in the USA (USD), with subsidiaries in the UK (GBP) and Japan (JPY).
*   **Challenge:** The UK subsidiary sells software licenses to the Japan subsidiary. If both subsidiaries report their revenue to the USA parent, the total global revenue is artificially inflated because it includes internal sales. This is a classic "Intercompany Elimination" problem.

## The FCCS Solution

### 1. Intercompany (ICP) Dimension
GlobalTech enabled the Intercompany dimension in FCCS. 
*   When the UK sub records the sale, they do not just post it to "Software Revenue". They post it to the intersection of `Entity: UK_Sub`, `Account: Revenue`, `ICP: ICP_Japan_Sub`.
*   Conversely, Japan records an expense at `Entity: Japan_Sub`, `Account: COGS`, `ICP: ICP_UK_Sub`.

### 2. Currency Translation
FCCS handles the multi-currency complexity natively.
*   UK data is loaded in local currency (GBP).
*   FCCS uses the Monthly Average Rate (for P&L accounts) and the Month-End Rate (for Balance Sheet accounts) to automatically translate the GBP data into USD when rolling up to the Parent.
*   The CTA (Cumulative Translation Adjustment) is automatically calculated to balance the balance sheet due to changing FX rates.

### 3. Automated Eliminations
This is the core value of FCCS. Because the data was tagged with ICP partners, the system automatically eliminates the internal transaction.
*   At the `Global_Parent` entity level, FCCS looks at the `FCCS_Eliminations` consolidation member.
*   It sees UK reported $1,000 revenue from Japan, and Japan reported $1,000 expense from UK.
*   It generates an automatic elimination entry: -$1,000 to Revenue, -$1,000 to Expense.
*   **Result:** The consolidated Net Income remains accurate, but the artificially inflated Revenue and COGS are removed from the global P&L.

## Outcome
By moving from manual Excel consolidations to FCCS, GlobalTech reduced their month-end close cycle from 15 days to 4 days, ensuring full IFRS audit compliance through automated, traceable eliminations.
