## 🚨 Business Problem

A mid-size manufacturing company manages its annual budget using 150+ Excel spreadsheets across 5 plants. Issues:
- Budget consolidation takes 6 weeks
- No variance analysis until month-end
- Plant managers submit budgets in different formats
- No audit trail — CFO can't trace who changed what
- Currency issues with export operations (USD, EUR)

## 📌 EPM Solution Design

### Application: ManufacturingPlan (EPBCS Freeform)

**Cubes:** FinPlan (BSO), CapitalPlan (BSO), Reporting (ASO)

### Dimension Design

| Dimension | Dense/Sparse | Level 0 Members |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Account | Dense | 300 (Revenue: 100xxx, Expense: 200xxx, BS: 300xxx) |
| Period | Dense | 12 months |
| Entity | Sparse | 5 plants + Corporate |
| Scenario | Sparse | Actual, Budget, Forecast |
| Version | Sparse | Working, Approved |
| Year | Sparse | FY25, FY26, FY27 |
| Product Line | Sparse | 15 product categories |
| Currency | Sparse | INR, USD, EUR |

### Key Forms
1. **Revenue Planning** — Units × Price per Product Line per Plant
2. **Raw Material Cost** — Bill of Materials × Volume
3. **Labor Cost** — Headcount × Salary per Department
4. **Capital Budget** — Asset purchases by plant
5. **P&L Summary** — Consolidated view

### Key Business Rules
1. Revenue = Units × Price (driver-based)
2. COGS = Raw Material + Direct Labor + Manufacturing Overhead
3. Currency Translation (INR → USD/EUR)
4. Plant Allocation (Corporate overhead → plants by revenue share)
5. Depreciation Calculation

### 💡 Logic & Purpose
Manufacturing requires multi-stage allocations. You cannot calculate Net Profit per plant until you have allocated the Corporate Overhead down to them. The Business Rules must be executed in a strict, sequential `Rule Set` (Calc Revenue -> Translate Currency -> Allocate Overhead -> Calc Profit) because Step 4 relies on the results of Step 1.

### 🌀 Confusion Resolution
> **Misconception:** "We have 15 product lines, so we should build an allocation script that uses `CALC ALL` at the end to ensure the total company profit is 100% accurate across all products."
> **Reality:** Never use `CALC ALL` in a production environment with multiple sparse dimensions (Plants, Products). It will force a full database restructuring calculation. You should use a targeted `AGG("Entity", "Product Line")` inside the rule set.

### 😈 Twisted Interview Question
**Question:** The plant allocation rule spreads the ₹10 Crore Corporate IT expense across the 5 plants based on their Revenue share. Plant A makes ₹0 Revenue this month because it was shut down for maintenance. The rule crashes with an "Essbase Error: 1200315 - Error parsing formula" during the execution for Plant A. Why?
> **Answer:** Divide by Zero error! The allocation logic is `(Plant Revenue / Total Revenue)`. If Total Revenue is 0 for a specific combination, or Plant Revenue is `#MISSING`, the equation explodes. An architect MUST wrap all allocation division logic in an `IF("Total Revenue" <> 0)` statement.

### Workflow
Plant Manager (Preparer) → Regional Head (Reviewer) → CFO (Approver)

## 📌 Results
- Budget cycle reduced from 6 weeks to 10 days
- Real-time variance analysis via dashboards
- Complete audit trail for compliance
- Multi-currency reporting for export operations

---

> **Interview Tip:** When discussing this case study, emphasize the driver-based planning approach (Units × Price) and how it enables what-if analysis (what if we increase price by 5%?).
