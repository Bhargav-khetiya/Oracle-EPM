# ⚡ Cheat Sheet: EPM Ecosystem

> **Quick revision before meetings or interviews**

---

| What | Answer |
|---|---|
| **EPM** | Enterprise Performance Management |
| **Purpose** | Plan, budget, forecast, report, consolidate |
| **Access** | Web Browser or Smart View (Excel add-in) |
| **Data Source** | ERP provides Actuals |
| **Engine** | Essbase (multidimensional database) |

**Evolution:** Hyperion (on-prem) → PBCS (basic cloud) → EPBCS (enhanced cloud)

**7 Pillars:**

| # | Abbreviation | Full Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EPBCS | Enterprise Planning & Budgeting | Planning & Budgeting |
| 2 | FCCS | Financial Consolidation & Close | Combining financials |
| 3 | ARCS | Account Reconciliation | Matching records |
| 4 | EDMCS | Enterprise Data Management | Master data |
| 5 | NRCS | Narrative Reporting | Reports + explanation |
| 6 | PCMCS | Profitability & Cost Management | Cost analysis |
| 7 | TRCS | Tax Reporting | Tax calculations |

---

### 💡 Pro Tip: EPBCS vs Custom
> **Misconception:** OOTB modules (EPBCS) are always better than Custom cubes.
> **Reality:** OOTB modules come with heavy pre-built baggage (rules, members) that you cannot easily delete. If your client's process doesn't naturally fit Oracle's OOTB design, trying to force it will destroy performance. A lean, Custom-built cube is often faster and easier to maintain for highly bespoke processes.
