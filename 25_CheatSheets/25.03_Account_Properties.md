### 💡 Pro Tip: Dynamic Calc Placement
> **Misconception:** Making all parent members `Dynamic Calc` saves disk space and improves performance.
> **Reality:** While true for *Dense* dimension parents (like high-level Accounts), making high-level *Sparse* dimension parents `Dynamic Calc` (like Total Entity) forces Essbase to dynamically build massive blocks in memory upon user retrieval. This causes extreme form load times and "grid max size" errors. High-level Sparse parents should usually be `Store` and calculated overnight.
