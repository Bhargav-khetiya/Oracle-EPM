## Approval Workflow States

| State | Who Can Act | Data Editable? | Next Action |
|-------|------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Not Started** | Owner (Planner) | ✅ Yes | Promote → 1st Pass |
| **1st Pass** | Owner | ✅ Yes | Promote → Submitted |
| **Submitted** | Reviewer | ❌ No | Approve or Reject |
| **Under Review** | Reviewer | ❌ No | Approve or Reject |
| **Approved** | Admin/CFO | ❌ No | Sign Off |
| **Rejected** | Owner | ✅ Yes (fix issues) | Re-promote |
| **Signed Off** | Nobody | ❌ Locked | — |

## Hotel Pvt Ltd Flow
```
GM enters budget → Promotes → Controller reviews → Approves →
CFO reviews All_Hotels → Signs Off → Period locked

Timeline: Budget entry (Oct) → Submit by Nov 15 → 
          Approve by Nov 25 → Sign Off by Nov 30
```

## Key Rules
- Approval is **per entity per scenario** — Mumbai can be approved while Delhi is still in progress
- **Rejection** sends entity back to owner with mandatory comments
- **Sign Off** = permanent lock — only admin can unlock
- Parent entity (All_Hotels) auto-promotes when all children approved
