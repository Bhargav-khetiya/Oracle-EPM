## 5. EXAM DAY TIPS

```
Before:
  ✅ Test your webcam and internet (if online proctored)
  ✅ Have government ID ready
  ✅ Clear desk of all materials
  ✅ Close all other applications

During:
  ✅ Read EVERY word — Oracle questions have subtle traps
  ✅ "Select ALL that apply" → usually 2-3 answers
  ✅ "BEST answer" → all options may be partially correct
  ✅ Flag uncertain questions → review at end
  ✅ Don't spend > 2 minutes per question
  ✅ 55 questions ÷ 90 minutes = ~1.6 minutes each

After:
  ✅ Results are immediate (pass/fail)
  ✅ Certificate available in CertView within 48 hours
  ✅ Add to LinkedIn: Oracle Cloud Planning Professional
```
