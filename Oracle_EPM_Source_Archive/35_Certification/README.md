### 💡 Pro Tip: Certification vs Experience
> **Misconception:** "Passing the 1Z0 certification proves I am a senior EPM consultant."
> **Reality:** The certification tests your knowledge of Oracle's official documentation and button placements. Real-world architecture—like diagnosing block explosions, writing memory-safe Groovy, or tuning Hybrid BSO—is rarely covered on the exam. Use the certification to get past HR filters, but rely on architectural mastery (like the Masterclass modules) to pass the technical interview.
