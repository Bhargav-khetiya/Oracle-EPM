## Schedulable Job Types

| Type | Examples | Typical Schedule |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Business Rule | CALC_PnL, AGG_Entity | On-demand or daily |
| Ruleset | RS_Monthly_Close | Monthly |
| Data Import | DL_Revenue_Actuals | Daily or monthly |
| Maintenance | Restructure DB, Clear Cube | Weekly |
| Export Snapshot | Backup | Weekly |

## Best Practices
- Schedule heavy jobs **outside business hours** (2-5 AM)
- Always enable **failure notifications**
- Use EPM Automate for complex sequences (scheduling UI is single-job only)
- Don't schedule overlapping jobs on same cube
