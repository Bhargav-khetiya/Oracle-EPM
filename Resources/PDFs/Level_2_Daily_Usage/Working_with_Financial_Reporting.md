## 1. FR Web Studio vs. Modern EPM Reports <a name="fr-vs-reports"></a>

Oracle is actively deprecating **Financial Reporting (FR)** (`Reports -> Financial Reporting`) in favor of **EPM Reports (`Reports -> Reports`)**. However, thousands of existing enterprise financial packages still rely on FR grids.

---

## 2. Viewing, Exporting & Dynamic Prompts <a name="viewing-and-exporting"></a>

- **Dynamic Prompts:** When opening an FR report, users select POV parameters (`Year`, `Period`, `Entity`).
- **Export Options:** Supports `HTML`, `PDF`, and `Excel (Fully Formatted Grid)`.

---

## 3. Report Books & Batch Bursting <a name="books-and-bursting"></a>

- **Books:** Compiles dozens of individual FR reports into a single consolidated, paginated PDF/Excel binder with automated Table of Contents generation.
- **Bursting:** Automatically runs and emails specific department profit/loss slices to 500 individual Cost Center managers overnight based on a security distribution table.
