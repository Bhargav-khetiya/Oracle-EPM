## 1. Strategic Workforce Planning (`SWP`) vs. Standard Workforce Module <a name="swp-vs-workforce"></a>

While the standard **Workforce Module** (`Financials -> Workforce`) focuses on 1-3 year budget compensation calculation (`Salary + FICA + Medical`), **Strategic Workforce Planning (`SWP`)** is a long-range (`3-5 year`) human capital strategy engine.

---

## 2. Demand vs. Supply Headcount Modeling & Attrition Drivers <a name="demand-supply"></a>

- **Demand Modeling:** Predicts required future headcount based on corporate strategic growth drivers (`e.g., Every $5M increase in Revenue requires +3 Customer Support Engineers`).
- **Supply & Attrition Modeling:** Models natural employee turnover (`Historical Attrition Rate = 12% per year`) and retirement age distribution.
- **Gap Analysis:** Alveo/SWP engine compares Demand against Supply to highlight exact headcount deficits by job role and year!

---

## 3. Competency & Skills Gap Analysis <a name="skills-gap"></a>

- Allows HR planners to map skills (`Cloud Architecture, AI Engineering, SAP ABAP`) to job roles and model upskilling / training costs vs external hiring.
