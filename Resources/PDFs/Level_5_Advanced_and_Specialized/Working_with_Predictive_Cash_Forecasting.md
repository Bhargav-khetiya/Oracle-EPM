## 1. Daily Cash Management Dashboard & Bank Account POV <a name="daily-cash"></a>

Treasury analysts open `Predictive Cash -> Daily Cash Forecast`. The dashboard displays rolling daily closing cash balances across operating and payroll bank accounts.

---

## 2. Overriding AI Daily Cash Predictions & Adding Explanations <a name="overrides"></a>

- If the AI predicts a $\$1.2\text{M}$ customer inflow on Wednesday, but the treasury analyst knows the customer requested a 5-day payment extension, the analyst enters a manual override in the `Adjusted Forecast` column and attaches an mandatory **Cell Note**.

## 3. Submitting Daily Treasury Forecasts & Variance Tracking <a name="treasury-forecasts"></a>
