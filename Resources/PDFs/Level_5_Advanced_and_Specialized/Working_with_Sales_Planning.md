## 1. Quota Negotiation & Territory Acceptance Workflows <a name="quota-negotiation"></a>

Field sales directors open `Sales Planning -> Quota Planning` to review assigned top-down targets from the VP of Sales. They can accept the target (`Promote`) or submit a counter-proposal with specific territory justifications.

---

## 2. Key Account & Product-Level Sales Modeling <a name="key-account"></a>

- Planners model revenue across major strategic accounts (`Key Accounts = Top 20 Customers`).
- Driver formulas calculate expected discounts, volume rebates, and gross profit contributions.

---

## 3. Pipeline Commitment & Probability-Weighted Forecasting <a name="pipeline-commit"></a>

- **Commit vs. Upside vs. Pipeline:** Sales reps categorize open CRM opportunities. Driver calculations multiply open opportunity value by historical stage probability (`Commit = 90%`, `Upside = 50%`) to generate expected quarterly closing revenue.
